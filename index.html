Inheritance odds for a future child

Choose each parent’s result for a gene, and see the chances for their child. The Punnett square shows the four equally likely combinations; the bars add them up.

Educational tool, not medical advice. Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored. For real decisions, talk to a genetic counsellor.

Adult height

A complex trait: shaped by hundreds of genes and nutrition. This uses the classic mid-parental formula, so treat it as a rough range.

What this page leaves out on purpose

  • An overall survival chance. No scientific model can give this from parental DNA, so the page shows condition-by-condition outlook instead.
  • Polygenic risk scores for diabetes, heart disease and similar conditions. They are weak, and less accurate for Indian ancestry.
  • The child’s sex. Predicting it is restricted under India’s PCPNDT Act.
  • New mutations. Every child carries a few changes neither parent has.
  • Eye colour, hair colour and skin tone. They depend on many genes, so a simple inheritance table would mislead.
